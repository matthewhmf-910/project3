# Green, Slowly: 3 Viral Video Prompts

These ideas come from the 10-shot Chanoma storyboard (`green_slowly_storyboard.html`). I ran the director's motion-plan brief in `motion_direction_plan.md` on each one. Each idea has three parts: a motion plan, a review of the plan, and a final prompt you can paste into a video generator.

**Product to keep accurate in every shot:** a white tulip-shaped cup on a white saucer holds a matcha latte. The latte art is a three-heart rosetta with a pulled stem. A slice of baked cheesecake with a caramelised edge sits on a small white plate, and both are on a round light-oak table. Behind them are a moss garden and old weathered timber. The street entrance is a dark wooden gate under a rust-brown tiled roof, set in a white plaster wall with a grey tile cap and a slim blue vertical sign.

**How to use these prompts**
- Upload Photo A (the table) and Photo B (the gate) as image references. Then the cup, latte art and gate stay recognisable.
- Video models handle on-screen text badly, so every prompt asks for a clean frame. Add the kinetic type from the storyboard in your editor. Each idea lists its type overlay separately.
- Some tools cap clips at 8 seconds. If yours does, generate two clips and join them at the cut marked ✂.

## Idea 1: Through the Gate

**Hook:** a point-of-view walk from a Tokyo side street, through an old gate, to the cup. No hard cut until the cup lands, so it feels like one continuous step inside. This uses the "come with me" format, and the last frame loops back into the first.

**Storyboard source:** shots 02, 03, 06 and 09.

### Motion plan

| Time | What the audience sees | What moves | Camera | Direction and speed | Into the next shot | Why |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0.0–1.5 | Wet stone pavement, white plaster wall, dark timber gate under a tiled roof | A ginkgo leaf drifts across the lens | Low gimbal glide, 30 cm off the ground | Forward at walking pace, steady | The gate post fills the foreground | Starting low and already moving makes the viewer feel they're walking in |
| 1.5–3.0 | Passing under the gate roof: the café's glass front with warm light inside | Umbrellas in the stand sway slightly | Same glide, rising to eye level | Forward, easing up 20% | The dark timber post wipes the frame left to right | The real object does the transition, so no effect is needed |
| 3.0–4.5 | Round oak table, garden through the glass | A hand enters from the right and sets the cup down | Glide slows to a stop exactly as the saucer touches | Forward, then decelerates to zero | The camera's stop and the cup landing hit together: hard cut | The first stop in the video falls on the product |
| ✂ 4.5–6.5 | Top-down on the latte: the three-heart rosetta | Foam settles and trembles from the set-down | Overhead, slow vertical descent | Down, very slow, about 10 cm over 2 s | Hard cut on the beat | The latte art is the hook frame, so it gets two full seconds |
| 6.5–8.5 | Three-quarter hero: cup in front, cheesecake and a second latte soft behind, moss garden beyond | Steam curls; a leaf drops outside | Locked off | None | Focus shifts from the cup to the garden | Holding still after all that movement makes the product feel calm, which is the brand |
| 8.5–10.0 | The garden's stone path, now in focus | Light rain on the moss | Locked off, then a 0.5 s forward creep | Forward, slow | The stone path matches the opening pavement and loops to 0.0 | A seamless loop gets replays |

**Review**
- **Rhythm:** a continuous glide for 4.5 s, then two still frames, then a creep into the loop. It goes fast-feeling, then calm, then loops, with no repeated zooms.
- **Continuity:** forward movement never reverses. The only cut lands on the cup set-down.
- **Product visibility:** the cup is on screen from 3.0 s to 8.5 s, and the latte art is full-frame for 2 s.
- **Impact:** the reveal is earned. The viewer walks in before seeing the cup.

### Final prompt

```
Vertical 9:16, 10 seconds, 24fps, photoreal cinematic commercial, one continuous point-of-view journey into a tiny Tokyo matcha café.

0–1.5s: Low gimbal shot 30cm above wet grey stone pavement on a quiet Tokyo side street, gliding forward at walking pace toward an old dark-timber gate with a rust-brown tiled roof, set in a white plaster wall with a grey tile cap and a slim blue vertical sign. A single yellow ginkgo leaf drifts across the lens in the foreground.
1.5–3s: The camera passes under the gate roof, rising to eye level. The dark timber gate post sweeps across the foreground left to right like a natural wipe, revealing a glass café front glowing with warm light, umbrellas in a ceramic stand.
3–4.5s: Inside, the forward glide decelerates to a full stop at a round light-oak table by a window onto a moss garden. Exactly as the camera stops, a hand sets down a white tulip-shaped cup on a white saucer holding a matcha latte.
4.5–6.5s: Hard cut to a top-down overhead view of the latte: vivid green matcha with white three-heart rosetta latte art and a pulled stem. The foam trembles from the set-down. The camera descends very slowly toward the surface.
6.5–8.5s: Hard cut to a locked-off three-quarter hero shot: the cup low in frame, a slice of baked cheesecake with a caramelised edge and a second café latte softly out of focus behind, the moss garden and weathered timber beyond. Thin steam curls up. Total stillness except steam.
8.5–10s: Slow rack focus from the cup to the wet stone garden path outside; light rain on moss; a slight final forward creep so the last frame matches the opening pavement for a seamless loop.

Soft overcast daylight, natural colour, true-to-life matcha green, warm oak, neutral white porcelain, shallow depth of field, 35mm look, fine grain. Clean frames with negative space in the upper third for text added in post. Audio: footsteps on wet stone, a gentle door chime at 3s, porcelain set on wood, soft rain, light lo-fi beat.
Avoid: on-screen text, logos, distorted hands, extra cups, changing latte art pattern, camera shake, random zooms, orbiting, morphing objects.
```

**Type overlay in the edit:** vertical チャノマ on the white wall (0.5 s), *slow* behind the cup (6.5 s), "Save for a slow afternoon" (9 s).

## Idea 2: Three Hearts (macro ASMR)

**Hook:** satisfying process video. The first frame is a single milk drop hitting matcha, and the rosetta then forms heart by heart. The camera barely moves, so the liquid does the work. This is the "oddly satisfying" format, and its sound gets people to turn audio on.

**Storyboard source:** shots 01, 04, 05 and 06.

### Motion plan

| Time | What the audience sees | What moves | Camera | Direction and speed | Into the next shot | Why |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0.0–1.0 | Extreme macro of a green matcha surface | One milk drop lands and a ring ripples outward | Locked overhead | None, slow-motion action | The ripple's circle matches the cup rim in the next shot | Something moves in frame 1 with no context, which stops the scroll |
| 1.0–3.0 | Side-on at rim height: the milk ribbon falling into the cup, backlit | The ribbon wobbles and the green surface swells | Slow pedestal up along the ribbon | Up, slow, constant | The ribbon thins and the pitcher lifts out of frame upward | Vertical movement suits the 9:16 frame |
| ✂ 3.0–5.5 | Top-down: three hearts form one after another, then the stem pulls through | The pitcher wiggles and the white pushes through green | Locked overhead | None, real time | The finished art fills the frame, then cut | The payoff. A still camera keeps the art readable |
| 5.5–6.5 | The cup on its saucer, top-down | The cup rotates 90° on a turntable and stops with the stem upright | Locked | Clockwise, eases to a stop | The rotation stops on the downbeat: hard cut | Shows off the art and lands on the beat |
| 6.5–8.0 | Low macro at saucer level | A spoon drops onto the saucer and bounces once | Lateral truck left, saucer rim in the foreground for parallax | Left, slow | The spoon settles and the camera keeps trucking into the hero | The sound peak of the clip |
| 8.0–10.0 | Three-quarter hero with cheesecake behind | Steam, plus the foam's light glint | Slow 10° arc left, then hold for the final 1 s | Left, slow, decelerates | Freezes on the hero for the cover frame | The final frame doubles as the post's thumbnail |

**Review**
- **Rhythm:** a slow-motion drop, a slow rise, a locked process shot, a quick rotation, then a glide that settles to stillness. Only two camera moves are real moves.
- **Continuity:** the ripple circle matches the cup rim, and the camera's truck carries into the arc.
- **Product visibility:** the latte is in every shot, and the cheesecake shows up for the reveal at 8 s.
- **Impact:** the ASMR sound and the rosetta forming make people rewatch. The spoon clink is the sound you remember.

### Final prompt

```
Vertical 9:16, 10 seconds, 24fps, photoreal macro food commercial, ASMR, satisfying latte art process.

0–1s: Extreme macro, locked overhead: a smooth vivid-green matcha surface in a white cup. A single drop of steamed milk falls and lands in slow motion, sending one perfect circular ripple outward.
1–3s: Side view at rim height, backlit by soft window light: a glossy ribbon of steamed milk pours from a steel pitcher into the matcha in slow motion; the camera rises slowly and smoothly along the ribbon.
3–5.5s: Locked top-down view: the barista's pitcher wiggles and three white hearts bloom one after another in the green, then a thin stem pulls through them, forming a three-heart rosetta. Real-time speed, crisp detail.
5.5–6.5s: Top-down: the white tulip-shaped cup on a white saucer rotates a quarter turn on a light-oak table and stops with the stem pointing straight up.
6.5–8s: Low macro at saucer height: a small silver spoon drops onto the saucer and bounces once; the camera trucks slowly left with the saucer rim passing in the foreground.
8–10s: Three-quarter hero shot: the finished matcha latte low in frame, a slice of baked cheesecake with a caramelised edge on a small white plate softly behind, moss garden and weathered timber through the window. Slow 10-degree arc left, settling into a still final frame.

Soft natural daylight, true-to-life matcha green, white foam, neutral porcelain, warm oak, very shallow depth of field, 100mm macro look, high detail on foam texture.
Audio, close-miked ASMR: milk drop, pour, foam hiss, porcelain turn, bright spoon clink at 6.5s, quiet café room tone. No music, or a very soft single piano note at 8s.
Avoid: on-screen text, logos, hands with extra fingers, latte art changing after it forms, foam boiling, camera shake, zoom pulses, orbiting.
```

**Type overlay in the edit:** POUR stretched either side of the ribbon (1 s), a ring of text on the rim, THREE HEARTS · ONE STEM (5.5 s), *poured, not printed* (8.5 s).

## Idea 3: Loud City, Quiet Cup (pattern interrupt)

**Hook:** five seconds of fast city cuts, then a sudden three-second hold on rain and moss with no music. The contrast is the joke: the quiet is the product. Sharp tempo changes keep people watching, and the last frame loops back into the noise.

**Storyboard source:** shots 01, 02, 07, 08, 09 and 10.

### Motion plan

| Time | What the audience sees | What moves | Camera | Direction and speed | Into the next shot | Why |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0.0–1.0 | A white high-rise apartment block | Nothing; the camera does the work | Fast tilt down from the tower to the old gate below | Down, fast, with motion blur | Lands on the gate on the beat | Shows the contrast in one move: tower, then wooden gate |
| 1.0–2.0 | The white plaster wall | The blue vertical sign whips through the foreground | Fast lateral track right | Right, fast | Motion blur carries into a hard cut | Parallax and speed |
| 2.0–3.0 | The cup set down hard on the oak table | Foam jumps and a single drop splashes on the saucer | Locked low angle | None; the action is fast | Hard cut | The product arrives with impact |
| 3.0–4.0 | The cheesecake slab | Its plate slides in from frame left and stops dead | Locked side-on | Left to right, fast | Hard cut | A graphic horizontal move after a vertical one |
| ✂ 4.0–5.0 | Overhead: matcha, café latte and cheesecake in a triangle | Nothing on the table moves | Overhead crane rotating 20° | Clockwise, fast, stops sharply | A sudden cut to silence | The energy peaks right before the drop |
| 5.0–8.5 | The moss garden from table height: rain dripping from the tiled eave, the cup soft in the foreground | Only raindrops and one leaf | Locked off | None for 3.5 s | Raindrop hits on the downbeat: hard cut | The pattern interrupt. Silence after noise makes people stop |
| 8.5–10.0 | Top-down: the three-heart latte centred on oak | Steam only | Locked overhead | None | Loops to the fast tilt at 0.0 | A clean end frame for the CTA overlay, and a loud loop restart |

**Review**
- **Rhythm:** five one-second cuts at full speed, a 3.5 s hold, and a 1.5 s end card. It is the most aggressive tempo contrast of the three ideas.
- **Continuity:** every fast shot's movement direction hands off to the next (down, right, set-down, slide right, rotate). The stillness is a deliberate break.
- **Product visibility:** the latte appears four times and the cheesecake twice, and the final frame is pure product.
- **Impact:** a strong first second (tower to gate), a memorable drop, and a loop that hits loud again.

### Final prompt

```
Vertical 9:16, 10 seconds, 24fps, photoreal editorial commercial with a sharp tempo change: five fast cuts, then sudden stillness.

0–1s: Fast tilt down from a white Tokyo high-rise apartment block to an old dark-timber gate with a rust-brown tiled roof below it, motion blur, landing sharply.
1–2s: Fast lateral track right along a white plaster wall with a grey tile cap; a slim blue vertical street sign whips through the foreground.
2–3s: Low locked angle on a round light-oak table: a white tulip-shaped cup of matcha latte on a white saucer is set down firmly; the green foam jolts and one small drop hits the saucer.
3–4s: Side-on locked shot: a slice of baked cheesecake with a caramelised golden edge on a small white plate slides in from frame left and stops dead in centre.
4–5s: Overhead: matcha latte, café latte and cheesecake arranged in a triangle on the round oak table; the camera rotates quickly 20 degrees clockwise and stops sharply.
5–8.5s: Sudden total stillness. Locked-off shot from table height looking into a small moss garden with weathered timber; rain drips from a tiled eave; the matcha cup is soft and out of focus in the foreground. Nothing else moves for three and a half seconds.
8.5–10s: Locked top-down view of the matcha latte centred on the oak table: vivid green with a white three-heart rosetta. Only faint steam moves.

Overcast daylight, natural colour, crisp contrast in the fast section, softer and quieter in the still section, true-to-life matcha green, 28mm for street shots, 50mm for table shots, shallow depth of field.
Audio: driving 120 BPM beat with city ambience for 0–5s, cut to complete silence at 5s, then only rain, a bird, and room tone; a single soft porcelain ting at 9.5s.
Avoid: on-screen text, logos, people's faces, camera shake, zooms, orbiting, slow motion in the first five seconds, latte art changing between shots.
```

**Type overlay in the edit:** MATCHA slamming in one letter per beat (0–1 s), + *cake* (3 s), FOR TWO (4 s), *breathe.* (6 s), wordmark and "Save for a slow afternoon" (8.5 s).
