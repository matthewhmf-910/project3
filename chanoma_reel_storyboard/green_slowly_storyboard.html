Production notes

What to shoot, how to set type, and how to deliver.

To shoot

  • 04 · The ribbon. Macro at rim height, 120 fps, backlit so the milk glows. 6–8 pours.
  • 05 · Rosetta spin. Overhead arm, cup on a turntable. Pour fresh art each take.
  • 08 · Table for two. Overhead flat-lay on the same round oak table. Slider or gimbal rotate.
  • All other frames can be graded and cut from Photos A and B for a stills-only version.

Type system

  • Loud words in a condensed grotesk at 900 weight: MATCHA, POUR, the wordmark.
  • Soft words in a serif italic: slow, breathe, cake.
  • Facts in small mono caps: address, frame rate, menu tags.
  • Japanese set vertically, quoting the street sign.

Sound

  • 120 BPM lo-fi house. 2 beats per shot, cut on the downbeat.
  • Foley carries the café: spoon, porcelain, gravel, door chime, pour.
  • Drop the music for shot 09 only, then re-enter on the end-card kick.

Delivery

  • 1080 × 1920, H.264, 24 fps, 10.0 s, seamless loop.
  • Safe zones. Keep type clear of the top 12%, bottom 22% and right-hand button rail. Toggle the overlay above to check.
  • Grade. Lift the greens toward the matcha, keep the timber warm and the porcelain neutral.

Safe-zone overlay is approximate; Instagram's UI varies by device. Placeholder wordmark set in the storyboard typeface.
