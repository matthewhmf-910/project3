# Tea × Cocktail: 10 s story and mapping

This follows the grammar in `style-bible.md`, using our own content, colours and copy.
Format: 720×1280, 24 fps, 240 frames, 132 BPM (beat 0.4545 s, first beat 0.10 s).

## Research notes

- The venue in the photos is **離念・茶蒔**, a Hong Kong tea concept store (signage: "Restaurant ·
  Fine Tea · Tea Cocktail"). It has a tea-in-cooking restaurant, a tea space for
  ceremonies and tea-cocktail sessions, takeaway tea drinks (e.g. the layered "Floating"
  espresso-over-tea-oat-milk) and a retail shelf of house teas and tins.
- In the wider context, Hong Kong bars (Tell Camellia, Quinary and others) have normalised
  "teatails". Tea-coffee fusion is a growing mainland trend.
- What this means for the story: the venue is **tea by day, cocktails by night**, and the
  product (tea, tins) is the through-line.

## Concept

The reference splits the frame into an *input sketch* and an *output animation*. We keep
that split, but the input card is **real photos from the store** and the output is an
illustrated one-shot animation. A **tea leaf** is the guide character and causes every
transformation.

## Story (START → TRANSFORMATION → END)

| # | START | TRANSFORMATION | END |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | A tea leaf falls in, filling the frame | The camera pulls back to a birch counter while a cream katakuchi bowl slides up | Bowl centred and empty |
| 2 | The leaf lands on a sieve above the bowl | Three taps, one per beat, each send a puff of matcha that lands as a lumpy clump | Bowl holds messy powder |
| 3 | The camera pushes in; the leaf perches on the rim and casts a warm light cone | Under the cone, each clump whisks into a clean concentric jade ring, one per beat | A smooth, foamed matcha surface |
| 4 | The leaf whips off (smear) | The bowl settles with a squash | A beat of stillness |
| 5 | Top-view bowl | The rim flattens into a glass rim and a ribbed glass grows downward; oat milk, matcha and amber tea layers rise, one per beat | A layered tea glass with the leaf riding the rim |
| 6 | Bright birch counter | Iris light switch (3 frames) | Night bar: oxblood, neon bokeh |
| 7 | Layered glass | It morphs into a stemmed cocktail glass and the liquid turns amber; a violet flower drops in; the leaf stamps the surface and ripples form a red ring | Tea cocktail with a seal ring on its surface |
| 8 | The seal ring | It rises out as a hammered-silver tea-tin lid stamped 茶; the glass exits up with blur; the camera pushes in ~2.2× | Hero: the tin lid with the leaf perched like a crown, held to the end |
